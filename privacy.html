Privacy Policy

Blockvile Plus OS · Last updated: [date you publish]

Blockvile Plus OS (“Blockvile”, “the app”) is a home screen and app collection for Android and the web. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: Blockvile has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not receive your data. What you create stays on your device.

Information kept on your device

These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to the developer:

  • Settings (theme, wallpaper, PIN, device mode, dock and home-screen layout)
  • Files, notes, reminders, calendar events, alarms and contacts you type into Blockvile
  • Photos you take or add, drawings, music you add and voice recordings
  • Your Cloud Music library and saved videos, your watch history, and your YouTube API key if you add one

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Erase everything & reset, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions and why they're used

Services Blockvile connects to

Some features fetch information from other services directly from your device. Those services receive the normal technical information any web request includes (such as your IP address) and follow their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo (weather): the city you search for, or your approximate coordinates if you tap “My location”. open-meteo.com
  • OpenStreetMap (maps and place search): place names you search for and the map area you view. osmfoundation.org
  • YouTube (Videos): YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player loads when you watch a video, and video titles are looked up through noembed.com. If you add your own YouTube API key, searches are sent to the YouTube Data API. Google Privacy Policy
  • SoundCloud (Cloud Music): SoundCloud's player loads when you play a link. soundcloud.com
  • Google Play: when you tap Install or search for Android apps, Blockvile opens Google Play, which handles the install. Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube (Videos app): YouTube's player loads when you play a video. If you add your own YouTube API key for in-app search, your searches go to Google's YouTube service. YouTube privacy
  • Feedback: only when you press Send in the Feedback app. Your message, rating, optional email and (if you tick the box) basic device details go to the address or form service set up by the publisher.
  • Websites you choose in the Browser or as web apps.

Blockvile does not sell, rent or share your information, and contains no advertising or tracking code.

Children

Blockvile is not directed at children under 13 and includes an unrestricted web browser.

Security

The optional screen PIN protects the Blockvile screen from casual access. It is stored on your device and is not a replacement for your phone's own lock screen.
