Privacy Policy

Blockvile Plus OS · Last updated: [date you publish]

Blockvile Plus OS (“Blockvile”, “the app”) is a home screen and app collection for Android and the web. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: no ads and no analytics. Without an account, what you create stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, the account details below are stored with the service that runs sign-in for this app (Supabase).

Information kept on your device

These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to the developer:

  • Settings (theme, wallpaper, PIN, device mode, dock and home-screen layout)
  • Files, notes, reminders, calendar events, alarms and contacts you type into Blockvile
  • Photos you take or add, drawings, music you add and voice recordings
  • Your Cloud Music library and saved videos, your watch history, and your YouTube API key if you add one

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Erase everything & reset, or by uninstalling the app.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in is optional and unlocks the Digital SIM, Blockvile AI answers and device linking. When you sign in, these are stored on the app's Supabase project (a hosted database), protected so that each account can only read its own data:

  • Your email address and name, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) if you use email sign-in. If you use Google or Microsoft sign-in, we receive your email and name from them. We never see your Google or Microsoft password.
  • The names of devices you link to your account (such as “Pixel 8”) and when they were last used.
  • Your Digital SIM: a Blockvile number, its charge level, its banked Wi-Fi data amount, and the text messages you send to or receive from other Blockvile numbers. The sender and recipient can read a message.
  • What you ask Blockvile AI is sent to the AI service to be answered; Blockvile does not keep it on the server. Your chat history is kept on your device only.

Google and Microsoft services: if you tap “Link … mail, calendar & files”, Google or Microsoft asks you to allow read-only access. Blockvile uses it only to show your unread mail count, next events and recent files on your own screen. That access is held in memory for the current visit only, is never saved or sent to the developer, and you can remove it any time in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

You can delete your account data by contacting us at the address below, and unlink devices yourself in Accounts. The Digital SIM is not a phone-company SIM and does not use your phone number.

Services Blockvile connects to

Some features fetch information from other services directly from your device. Those services receive the normal technical information any web request includes (such as your IP address) and follow their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo (weather): the city you search for, or your approximate coordinates if you tap “My location”. open-meteo.com
  • OpenStreetMap (maps and place search): place names you search for and the map area you view. osmfoundation.org
  • YouTube (Videos): YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player loads when you watch a video, and video titles are looked up through noembed.com. If you add your own YouTube API key, searches are sent to the YouTube Data API. Google Privacy Policy
  • SoundCloud (Cloud Music): SoundCloud's player loads when you play a link. soundcloud.com
  • Google Play: when you tap Install or search for Android apps, Blockvile opens Google Play, which handles the install. Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube (Videos app): YouTube's player loads when you play a video. If you add your own YouTube API key for in-app search, your searches go to Google's YouTube service. YouTube privacy
  • Feedback: only when you press Send in the Feedback app. Your message, rating, optional email and (if you tick the box) basic device details go to the publisher by e-mail through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co) and, if the publisher uses Supabase, are saved in its database.
  • The publisher's website (software updates): Blockvile asks it whether a newer version exists and downloads the new files. It receives the normal technical information any web request includes, such as your IP address.
  • Supabase (sign-in, devices, Digital SIM): only if you sign in. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google and Microsoft (sign-in and optional linking): only if you choose them. Google · Microsoft
  • Websites you choose in the Browser or as web apps.

Blockvile does not sell or rent your information, and contains no advertising or tracking code.

Children

Blockvile is not directed at children under 13 and includes an unrestricted web browser.

Security

The optional screen PIN protects the Blockvile screen from casual access. It is stored on your device and is not a replacement for your phone's own lock screen.
